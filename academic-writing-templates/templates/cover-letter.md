# 投稿 Cover Letter 模板

```text
Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "{Manuscript title}" for consideration in {Journal name}.

This study examines {research question} using {study design/data source}. The main finding is that {principal finding}, which may contribute to {field/practice/future research}.

We believe this manuscript may be of interest to readers of {Journal name} because:

1. {Reason 1: relevance to journal scope}
2. {Reason 2: methodological or clinical contribution}
3. {Reason 3: practical implication or evidence gap}

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. {Add ethics approval, consent, trial registration, data availability, funding, and conflict-of-interest statements as required by the journal.}

Thank you for considering our manuscript.

Sincerely,

{Corresponding author name}
{Affiliation}
{Email}
```

## Checklist

- 期刊名是否正确。
- 稿件标题是否与投稿系统一致。
- 是否包含伦理、基金、利益冲突、数据可用性声明。
- 是否避免夸大“首次”“突破性”等营销表达。
- 是否符合目标期刊字数和格式要求。
