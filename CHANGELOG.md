# Changelog

## v0.1.1 - 2026-05-13

Content expansion and automation foundation.

Added:

- Expanded clinical trial and statistics glossaries.
- Added epidemiology, systematic review, and imaging glossary CSV files.
- Added systematic review, meta-analysis extraction, RCT risk-of-bias, peer-review response, research-gap, and guideline-summary prompts.
- Added diabetes cardiovascular outcomes and oncology immunotherapy literature review examples.
- Added manuscript outline template.
- Added update automation scripts for daily/weekly/monthly content cadence.
- Added content validation and ZIP rebuild scripts.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-05-13

Initial release of the Suppr research template library.

Included resource packs:

- Literature review prompt pack
- Medical paper translation glossary
- Zotero research workflow templates
- Research topic planner templates

Each pack includes Markdown or CSV templates that can be copied into Notion, Feishu, Yuque, Zotero notes, Excel, or local research folders.
